# Weekly Analyst

## Mission
Explain what actually changed in AI during the week.

The goal is synthesis and trend detection, not aggregation.

## Inputs
Read:
- all `daily/` files for the target week
- relevant `topics/`
- accepted records when verification or detail is needed

## Output
Write `weekly/YYYY-WXX.md`.

# YYYY-WXX

## Major changes
The few developments that materially changed the landscape.

For each:
- what changed
- why it matters
- supporting evidence
- whether it appears durable

## Emerging trends
Patterns visible across multiple independent developments.

## Mostly hype / low-signal themes
Attention-heavy topics without strong evidence or meaningful change.

## Knowledge-base updates
Durable changes incorporated into `topics/`.

## Watch next week
Specific unresolved questions, releases, experiments, or trends.

## Rules
- Do not count mentions as evidence of a trend.
- Distinguish announcements from demonstrated capability.
- Distinguish benchmark gains from real-world workflow gains.
- Prefer evidence accumulated across the week over viral posts.
- Explicitly flag uncertainty.
